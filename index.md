I am **Xuning Hu**, an undergraduate student at the University of Liverpool. My research lies at the intersection of *Human-Computer Interaction* (HCI) and *Extended Reality* (XR), with a focus on identifying complex interaction tasks and enhancing user performance through computational modeling and the design of novel interaction techniques.

I have conducted research at the **FIT-AWE Lab** (supervised by [Prof. Hai-Ning Liang](https://cma.hkust-gz.edu.cn/people/hai-ning-liang/)), the **VVISE Lab** at Simon Fraser University (mentored by [Prof. Wolfgang Stuerzlinger](https://vvise.iat.sfu.ca/people/wolfgang-stuerzlinger)), and the **Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences** (under [Prof. Jin Huang](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~huangjin)). In addition, I have actively collaborated with several international researchers, including [Prof. Yuntao Wang](https://pi.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/lab/people/YuntaoWang/en/) (Tsinghua University), [Prof. Dominic Kao](https://polytechnic.purdue.edu/profile/kaod) (Purdue University), Rajkumar Darbar (CNRS, France), and [Prof. Yukang Yan](https://rochester-bear-lab.github.io/) (University of Rochester), with whom I continue to maintain close academic collaboration.
